## Statement

The platform shall provide APIs and protocols for connecting multiple APTL instances into federated ranges for large-scale exercises, supporting centralized, decentralized, or hybrid federation models.

## Rationale

NATO Locked Shields federates 40+ countries' ranges. Springer 2026 research identifies centralized, decentralized, and hybrid federation models with tradeoffs in governance, autonomy, and interoperability. Federation enables exercises beyond single-organization scale.
