Evidence capture keeps complete source content. It no longer cuts, prefixes, or caps evidence by size or count. Content streams to the run store in constant memory. The new `run_storage.free_space_reserve_percent` setting (default 10) and `run_storage.capture_progress_timeout_seconds` setting (default 1800) replace the per-capture byte, count, and duration limits. `CaptureLimits` is removed from the family adapter interface.
